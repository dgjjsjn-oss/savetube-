How to Use SaveTube

Step-by-step guide and common questions

Download a video in three steps

  1. Copy the video link. Open the video on YouTube and copy the address from the browser bar. It looks like youtube.com/watch?v=.... The Share button works too.
  2. Paste it into the box and press Get Link. The video's title, channel, and available quality levels appear. If you pasted a channel link instead of a video link, the site will tell you.
  3. Pick your format and unlock. Choose a video resolution or an audio bitrate. Complete the quick one-step unlock, then press Download. The file is saved by your browser to your Downloads folder.

Frequently asked questions

Is SaveTube free?

Yes. The site is free and supported by advertising. You never need an account, and you never need to pay.

Do I need to install anything?

No. SaveTube runs in your browser on desktop, tablet, and phone. There is no app, extension, or plugin.

What quality should I choose?

Only the qualities the original video has are offered. 1080p or 720p is a good balance for most people. 4K files are large and slower to prepare. For music, podcasts, or talks, choose audio only and pick 192 kbps or higher.

Why is the audio MP3 and not something else?

MP3 plays on almost every device and program, so it is the most compatible choice for audio.

Can I get a transcript?

Yes, for videos that publish subtitles. Open the Transcript tab after looking up a video. If the creator did not provide subtitles, no transcript is available.

Why does a video fail?

  • The video may be private, age-restricted, or blocked in your region.
  • The video may have been deleted or made members-only.
  • The server may be busy — wait a few seconds and try again.
  • A very long video in 4K takes longer to prepare. Try a lower quality.

What is the one-step unlock?

SaveTube is free because of advertising. Before a file is prepared, one short ad step is shown. Once it is done, the download runs normally for the rest of your visit.

Do you store the videos?

No. Files are prepared and sent to you, then removed from the server. We do not keep a library and we do not host other people's content.

Is it legal to download YouTube videos?

Downloading may be fine or may not be, depending on the video, your country, and the licence it was published under. Only download content you have the right to save — your own uploads, Creative Commons material, or content you have permission to copy. You are responsible for how you use the tool. See the Terms of Service.

Can I use this on my phone?

Yes. Open the site in your mobile browser, paste the link, and the file saves to your device like any other download.

The download did not start. What now?

Check that your browser is not blocking the download, allow pop-ups for this site, and try again. If the problem continues, tell us through the Contact page.

Still stuck?

Send us a message from the Contact page with the video link and what happened. We use every report to improve the site.
