About SaveTube

Last updated: September 2026

SaveTube is a free web tool that helps people save YouTube videos and audio for personal use. You paste a link, choose a quality, and the file is delivered to your device. There is no app to install, no account to create, and no software to update.

What the site does

  • Saves videos in the resolution you pick, from small files up to 4K where the source supports it.
  • Saves audio as MP3, from 64 kbps up to 320 kbps.
  • Shows a text transcript for videos that publish subtitles.
  • Works on desktop and mobile browsers.

How it works

When you paste a link, SaveTube reads the public information YouTube publishes about the video, such as its title, channel, and available quality levels. When you choose a format, the file is prepared and delivered to your browser as a normal download. We do not keep a library of videos and we do not host other people's content on the site.

Who it is for

SaveTube is made for everyday users: students saving a lecture, creators archiving their own uploads, people on slow connections who want to watch offline, and anyone who needs the audio from a talk or a song. The site is intended for personal, non-commercial use.

What we believe

Simple tools should be fast, free, and honest. That is why there is no forced sign-up, no bundle of confusing buttons, and no hidden catch. The site is supported by advertising instead of subscriptions, so it can stay free for everyone.

Your responsibility

Please only download content you have the right to save, such as your own videos, videos published under a permissive licence, or material you have permission to copy. SaveTube is not affiliated with YouTube or Google, and it is your responsibility to respect copyright and the terms of any service you use. If you are a rights holder and would like something removed, see our Copyright Policy.

Why the site exists

Most download tools online are built around confusion. They hide the real download button behind three others, push you toward a subscription you did not ask for, or fill the screen with pop-ups the moment the page opens. SaveTube was built the other way round: one input box, clear choices, and a file at the end. If a step is not needed, it is not there.

How the site is funded

SaveTube is free and always will be. Servers, bandwidth, and the processing power used to prepare files all cost money, and that cost is covered by advertising. We deliberately keep ads in fixed positions around the content rather than over it, so nothing is hidden behind a banner and no button is ever disguised as something it is not. You will never be asked to sign up, hand over a card, or install anything.

What we do with your data

Almost nothing. You do not need an account, so we have no profile, no email list, and no password database. Links you paste are used to fetch the public information needed to prepare your file and are held in memory briefly so repeat requests are fast. Video files are written to temporary storage, sent to you, and then deleted. There is no archive of downloads and no record of what any individual visitor saved. The full detail, including what cookies do and do not do, is in our Privacy Policy and Cookie Policy.

Technical notes

The front end is plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript with no tracking frameworks and no third-party JavaScript beyond advertising partners you consent to. If the download service is available on the domain you are using, files are prepared on that server and delivered to you directly. If it is not available, the site clearly says so and offers an alternative route rather than pretending to work.

Accessibility

SaveTube is built to be usable with a keyboard alone, with a screen reader, and on a phone. Colours are chosen to meet contrast guidance, the dark theme follows your device setting automatically, and motion is reduced when your system asks for that. If something does not work for you, please tell us and we will fix it.

Keeping information accurate

Guides and policy pages on this site are reviewed when the service changes and at least once a year. Each policy page shows the date it was last updated at the top. If a change affects your rights, privacy, or how advertising works, the date is changed and the new version applies from that date onward.

Contact

Questions, feedback, or a broken link? Visit the Contact page and send us a message. We read everything, and we reply to reports about copyright, privacy, and broken downloads first.
