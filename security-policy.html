Security & Anti-Phishing

How to tell the real SaveTube from a fake one, and how to report a clone.

1. Read this first: we will never ask for your password

SaveTube never asks you to sign in to Google, YouTube, Gmail, or any other account. Not to remove a download limit, not to unlock a quality setting, not to speed anything up, not ever.

There is no such thing as a "sign in to remove your download limit" on this site. A real downloader has no reason to touch your account, because there is no limit to remove. If any page claiming to be SaveTube asks you to log in, it is a phishing attack and you should close it immediately without entering anything.

The same applies to fake "player updates" and "your file is ready, click to download" pages that hand you an .exe, .apk or .dmg. SaveTube hands you media files - MP4, WebM, MP3, M4A. A web tool never needs to install software to give you a video.

2. How to check you are on the real site

A working download on SaveTube behaves like this:

  • You paste a link and press Start. The page stays on savetube and shows the video title, channel and thumbnail.
  • You pick a quality and the file downloads from this same site, named something like video-title - 720p.mp4.
  • There is no login step, no download counter, no "verification" page, and no file named converter.exe or similar.

Being redirected to another domain mid-download, or being asked to install anything, both mean you are on a fake.

3. Why clones exist, and what we do about them

The downloader niche is heavily impersonated. Fraudsters copy the name, the logo and the interface of established services to harvest Google logins, to deliver malware, or to charge for a "premium" unlock that does not exist. This is a real and common problem in this industry, and it is one of the reasons this page exists.

What we do about it:

  • This site publishes a machine-readable security.txt, the standard file that lets hosts, browsers and abuse reporters find the real operator fast.
  • We will send takedown notices and support abuse reports for lookalike domains and phishing pages using our name or logo.
  • We keep a plain statement on every page that we never ask for a login.

4. Reporting a fake or a scam

If you find a site pretending to be SaveTube - a clone, a phishing page, or an installer being passed off as a download - please report it. Include the exact address, a screenshot if you have one, and what it asked you to do.

  • Email us through the contact page with "Phishing report" in the subject.
  • You can also file it with your hosting provider using the security.txt contact address, which is the format abuse desks expect.

We cannot remove content from sites we do not control, but we can send the right notice to the right host, and we usually can.

5. How this site is protected

For anyone auditing the site rather than using it, here is what is actually in place:

  • No accounts, no passwords, no payment details. There is nothing on this site to steal, which is why a login request from a "SaveTube" page is always fake.
  • No analytics or third-party tracking of who you are. Ad networks may set their own cookies, but we do not profile visitors or log which links people download.
  • Your IP is not shared with the platforms you download from. Downloads are fetched by our own servers, so the video platform never learns your home address.
  • Strict Content-Security-Policy on every page, so injected third-party scripts cannot run.
  • Rate limiting and connection caps to blunt abuse and scraping.
  • Credential files are denied by name on the web server, so a cookie or key file can never be downloaded from this site even by accident.
  • The contact form is honeypot-protected and rate limited, so it cannot be turned into a spam relay.

6. Responsible use

SaveTube is provided for personal, lawful use: your own videos, Creative Commons material, public-domain works, and content you have permission to download. Do not use it to redistribute copyrighted work, and do not use it to break any law in your country.

7. Contact

Security reports and questions go through the contact page. For anything urgent, use the address in our security.txt.
