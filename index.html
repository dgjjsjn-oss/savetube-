SaveTube in plain words

What SaveTube does

SaveTube is a free web tool that saves YouTube videos and audio to your device. You paste a video link, choose the quality you want, and the file arrives as a normal download. There is nothing to install, no account to create, and no subscription to cancel. The site works in any modern browser on a computer, a tablet, or a phone.

Choosing a video quality

Where the video supports it you can save in 4K, 1440p, 1080p, 720p, 480p, 360p, or 144p. The list is built from what the video actually offers, so you only ever see formats that exist for that specific video. Higher numbers mean a sharper picture and a larger file; if you are on metered data or filling a small phone, a lower number downloads faster and takes up less space.

Saving just the audio

If you only want the sound, switch to the Audio tab and choose a bitrate between 64 kbps and 320 kbps. This gives you a small MP3 file that plays anywhere, which is useful for lectures, interviews, podcasts, and music you already have the right to keep. Lower bitrates make noticeably smaller files for speech; higher bitrates keep more detail in music.

Downloading only part of a video

Long videos often contain only one short section you need. Tick the box marked Download only part of this video and enter a start time, an end time, or both. Times can be typed as 0:30, 1:15, or 1:02:30. The file that arrives contains only that section, which means a smaller file and a much faster download. Leaving the end time empty saves everything from your start point to the end of the video.

Reading a video instead of watching it

The Transcript tab returns the spoken text of a video with timestamps. It is faster to skim than watching at double speed, and it is easier to quote, cite, or translate. Transcripts are generated from subtitles the video publishes, so videos without captions will not have one.

Dark mode

SaveTube follows your device automatically. If your phone or computer is set to dark, the site opens dark. If you prefer the other look, press the moon or sun button in the header and your choice will be remembered on that device. No setting is sent anywhere, because the choice is stored only in your own browser.

Before you download

Save files you have the right to save: your own uploads, videos released under a permissive licence, public domain material, or content you have permission to copy. SaveTube is not connected to YouTube or Google and does not host other people's videos. See the Disclaimer and Copyright Policy for the full position.

Common questions

Is SaveTube free?

Yes. Downloading is free and does not need an account. The site is paid for by the advertisements placed around the content, which is why there is no charge and no forced sign-up.

Do I have to install anything?

No. Everything runs in your browser. If the download service is active on this domain, your file is prepared on the server and sent to you directly, so there is no extension, no desktop app, and no update to manage.

Why does a video fail to load?

Private, deleted, age-restricted, and region-locked videos cannot be read, and neither can videos that block embedding. If the link opens in YouTube but not here, the most common causes are those. Live streams and premieres also cannot be saved until they have finished.

Can I download a whole playlist or a channel?

No. SaveTube handles one video at a time, which keeps the service fast for everybody and keeps it within the limits of a free tool. Paste a single video link each time.

What happens to the file after I download it?

It is written to temporary storage, sent to your browser, and then deleted from the server. Files are not kept in a library and there is no archive of what anyone downloaded. See the Privacy Policy for the detail.

Does SaveTube work on a phone?

Yes. The layout adapts to small screens and the same features are available, including quality selection and the timeline cut. Your file is saved wherever your browser normally saves downloads.
