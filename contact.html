Email: business.support.website@proton.me

Before you write

  • Check the How to use page — most questions are answered there.
  • Make sure the video is public and not age-restricted or private.
  • Age-restricted, private, and region-locked videos cannot be processed.

Please note: SaveTube is not affiliated with YouTube or Google. We cannot help with YouTube account issues, channel strikes, or content that other people uploaded.

How to write a report we can act on

A short, specific message gets resolved much faster than a long one. Including the details below means we do not have to write back and ask for them.

  • The full link to the video or page you are writing about, copied from your browser address bar.
  • The format you chose, for example video 720p or audio 192 kbps, and whether you used the timeline cut.
  • What happened, in one or two sentences: the page froze, the file would not open, the wrong quality arrived, and so on.
  • Your browser and device, for example Chrome on Windows or Safari on iPhone.
  • For copyright matters, the details listed in our Copyright Policy, including a statement that you own the rights or are authorised to act for the owner.

What we cannot do

Being clear about limits saves everyone time. SaveTube cannot process private, deleted, age-restricted, or region-locked videos, because the information needed to prepare a file is simply not available for them. We cannot bypass a paywall, a login, or a geographic block. We cannot remove content from YouTube, restore a deleted video, or resolve an account, monetisation, or community guideline issue, because those systems belong to YouTube and not to us. We also do not offer paid plans, priority queues, or account upgrades, so any message offering to sell you one did not come from us.

Data and privacy enquiries

Because SaveTube has no accounts, there is usually no personal record to look up. If you believe we hold information about you, or you want to exercise a right described in the Privacy Policy, write to us and say which right you are using. We answer within the time limits set by the applicable law, which is one month for requests made under the GDPR and 45 days for requests made under the CCPA.

Reporting a security issue

If you find a way to abuse the service, bypass a limit, or access something you should not be able to reach, we would like to know. Describe the issue and the steps to reproduce it, and please do not exploit it further, publish it, or use it to affect other visitors. We will confirm receipt, work on a fix, and credit you if you would like that.

Response times

We aim to reply to every message within two working days. Copyright and privacy requests are handled first, followed by anything that stops the site from working. Feedback and feature suggestions are read and logged, but a personal reply can take longer because they are lower priority than breakage and legal matters.

Official channel only

The only address we use is the one shown on this page. SaveTube has no other support accounts on social media, no chat widget, and no phone line. If somebody contacts you claiming to represent SaveTube and asks for payment, a password, or access to your device, it is not us. Please ignore it and let us know so we can warn others.
