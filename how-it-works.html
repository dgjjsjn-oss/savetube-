How SaveTube works

Three steps, a few seconds, done. No account, no install, no fuss.

Copy the link

Open any YouTube video, Short, or live stream. Tap Share and copy the link, or copy it straight from the address bar.

Paste and choose quality

Paste the link into the box on the home page. SaveTube reads the video in seconds and shows every quality available, from 4K down to small files, plus audio bitrates.

Download

Pick the quality you want and tap Download. The file starts immediately, ready to watch offline or keep forever.

Fast

Most downloads start within about 5 seconds. No queues, no waiting rooms.

Private

Videos are fetched on demand. Nothing is stored and nothing is tracked.
