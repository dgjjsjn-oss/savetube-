Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information SaveTube collects when you use the website, why we collect it, and how you can control it. By using the site you agree to the practices described below.

1. Information we collect

SaveTube is a lightweight web page. We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or payment information. The information that may be collected automatically includes:

  • Your IP address and approximate location (standard internet connection data).
  • Your browser type, device type, and operating system.
  • Pages you visit on the site and how long you stay.
  • The YouTube link you paste is processed only inside your own browser to show the video title and thumbnail. We do not store the link or the video content on our servers.

2. How we use information

We use this information for a few limited purposes: to keep the site loading correctly, to understand which pages visitors use so we can improve the site, and to display advertising that keeps the service free for everyone. We do not sell your personal information to anyone.

3. Advertising and third parties

The site may display ads provided by third-party advertising networks such as Adsterra or PropellerAds. These partners may use cookies, web beacons, or similar technologies to measure ad performance and to show ads that are relevant to you. They may collect information about your visits to this site and other sites over time. You can control personalized advertising through your browser settings or through the opt-out tools offered by advertising networks.

4. Download and transcript partners

When you choose a format, you are sent to a third-party service to complete the download, and the same applies to transcripts. Those services have their own privacy policies, and we encourage you to read them. We are not responsible for how those services handle your data.

5. Cookies

The site uses a small amount of cookies. Our cookie consent banner asks for your choice when you first visit, and you can read the full details on our Cookie Policy page. You can also block or delete cookies from your browser at any time.

6. Your choices

  • You can decline cookies through the consent banner or your browser.
  • You can clear your browser data at any time.
  • You can stop using the site at any time.

7. Children's privacy

SaveTube is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13, and we do not create profiles of children or target advertising at them. If you are under 13, please do not use the site.

Where local law sets a higher age of digital consent (for example 16 in parts of the European Union and the United Kingdom), users below that age should only use the site with a parent or guardian's permission.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has used SaveTube, contact us at business.support.website@proton.me and we will help you exercise your rights, including deletion of any data associated with their use.

8. Your rights (GDPR and CCPA)

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • Ask what personal data we hold about you and receive a copy.
  • Ask us to correct or delete that data.
  • Object to or restrict certain processing.
  • Withdraw consent for cookies and advertising at any time.
  • Opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information as those terms are used under California law.

We do not sell personal information. Advertising partners may use cookies to show personalised ads; you can refuse this with the cookie banner, change your choice any time with the "Cookie settings" link in the footer, or opt out of personalised advertising through your browser and industry tools such as youronlinechoices.com and optout.aboutads.info.

To make a request, email business.support.website@proton.me. We reply within 30 days.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, the date at the top of this page will change. We encourage you to check this page occasionally so you stay informed.

10. Contact

If you have questions about this policy, contact us at business.support.website@proton.me or through the Contact page.
