Privacy Policy

The short version: we collect almost nothing, we sell nothing, and we do not track you.

What we collect

When you use SaveTube we process the video link you paste in order to resolve and serve the file you asked for. That link is handled transiently and is not retained after the request completes.

What we do not do

We do not require accounts. We do not create profiles. We do not sell or rent personal data. We do not run cross-site trackers on our pages beyond standard advertising technologies described below.

Advertising

SaveTube may display advertising served by third-party networks such as Google AdSense. Those networks may use cookies or similar technologies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. You can opt out of personalized advertising at Google's Ads Settings, and you can control cookies through your browser.

Logs and security

Our host automatically collects technical logs (IP address, user agent, timestamp, requested URL) to operate the service, mitigate abuse, and enforce rate limits. Logs are kept for a short operational period and are not used to identify you personally.

Third-party links

Some pages include links to third-party sites. Their privacy practices are their own; check their policies before sharing anything with them.

Children

SaveTube is a general-audience service. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, we will update this page and the date below.
