1. Who runs this site

SaveTube is an independent, volunteer-built video downloader web application. It is not operated by, and has no affiliation with, YouTube, Google, TikTok, Meta, X Corp, Reddit, Vimeo, SoundCloud, Pinterest, Twitch, Bilibili, Dailymotion or any advertising network mentioned below. This policy covers only SaveTube itself. Each platform has its own privacy policy, and reading the source platform's policy is a good idea before you paste a link.

2. What we do not collect

These are not collected, stored, transmitted or sold by SaveTube:

  • No account. There is no registration, no login, no user database and no password.
  • No name or email. Nothing on the downloader asks who you are.
  • No video content. Files are streamed to you and are not written to our disks. We hold no library.
  • No browsing history. We do not track which sites you visit before or after SaveTube.
  • No link history. A link you paste is used to answer that one request and then forgotten.
  • No analytics profile. We do not build a profile of you across sites.

5. Advertising

SaveTube is funded by advertising. This is why it can be free and require no account.

We use Adsterra and its network of advertising partners. When an advertisement loads, our server's Content Security Policy permits the request to go through, and the advertising partner's own script runs in your browser. Because of that, the partner - not SaveTube - is the one able to read and set things in your browser during that interaction.

Commonly observed categories include:

  • Pop-unders and pop-ups. A new window or tab may open behind or in front of the page when you click. It closes immediately, does not replace the page you were on, and never stops a download.
  • Social bars. A bar may appear along an edge encouraging you to follow, share or install something.
  • In-page banners. A display advertisement inside the page, clearly labelled.

What an advertising partner may do is described in its own privacy policy, which we cannot write for it. In practice, partners in this category commonly:

  • read your IP address and user agent;
  • store and read their own cookies and similar identifiers;
  • measure whether an advertisement was shown, and whether it led anywhere;
  • in some cases, build an interest profile using data from other sites. SaveTube has no control over this and no visibility into it.

We do not send advertising partners your name, your email, the link you pasted, or any video you downloaded. We do not control, endorse or have any financial interest in the content of any advertisement, and we have not reviewed the third-party sites that advertisements may lead to.

If you would rather see no advertising at all, an ad blocker will prevent the scripts from loading. The downloader continues to work, the ad slots simply stay empty, and SaveTube earns nothing from that visit.

6. Server logs and your address

Our hosting provider (Render) passes the connecting network address so the server knows where to send the response. Where a request arrives through a proxy, the real client address is taken from the X-Forwarded-For header only when the site is explicitly configured to trust that proxy. Trusting an unverified header would allow anyone to forge an address and evade rate limits, so it is off unless deliberately enabled.

Your address is used for three things and nothing else:

  • Rate limiting. A ceiling on requests per minute and downloads per hour, so one visitor cannot exhaust the server for everyone else.
  • Automatic blocking. Repeated abuse results in a temporary block for that address.
  • Counting. Aggregate visit counts, which are not linked to an identity.

These counters are held in memory and reset when the server restarts.

7. Bug reports and contact messages

If you choose to send a bug report, it is stored so the developer can read it. A report may contain the page you were on, the platform you selected, the text you typed, your network address, your browser's user agent string and the page you came from.

Input is treated as hostile. Every field is stripped of HTML, URLs, email addresses, script-like content, control characters and zero-width characters before it is stored. This is not decoration: the form is public, so anything arriving through it is assumed to be an injection attempt. A report that contained only such content is rejected.

A hidden field and a minimum response time are used to reject automated submissions. These are dropped silently, so a bot receives no signal that it was detected.

Bug reports are limited to the most recent 200 and are visible only to the site operator. You can ask for yours to be deleted at any time using the contact details in section 17.

9. Who else can see your data

Only these parties receive information as a normal consequence of using this site:

  • Render, the hosting provider, which processes every request in order to serve the page and enforce network security.
  • The advertising networks listed in section 5, when and only when their scripts run.
  • The platform whose link you pasted. That platform receives a request to resolve the video, and applies its own policy. This is unavoidable and is the entire mechanism of the service.
  • Cloudflare, if and when this site is placed behind its network, which would then sit between you and the server for security purposes.

We do not sell, rent or trade personal data. We have never shared it for profit and we will not start.

10. Security

Traffic is encrypted in transit using HTTPS. The site sets a strict Content Security Policy that limits which external origins may load, so a compromised or injected script has nowhere useful to send data. Administrative and statistics endpoints require a secret token that is stored only in the server's environment and is never present in the page source. Input is length-capped, allow-listed and sanitised before storage.

No system is perfect. If a breach occurs that affects your data, we will correct it and, where the law requires, notify affected people and the relevant authority.

11. Your rights under the GDPR

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, you have the following rights.

Access

You may ask whether we hold data about you and receive a copy of it. In practice, the only data likely to exist is a bug report or a message you sent, which you will already have a copy of. Request it using section 17.

Rectification and erasure

You may ask us to correct something inaccurate, or to delete it entirely. We will act on both, usually immediately, because the data is either yours already or trivially deletable.

Objection and restriction

You may object to processing based on legitimate interests, or ask us to pause it. Because the processing described here is either necessary for the service to function or is something you actively submitted, an objection is handled by deleting the item.

Withdraw consent

You may withdraw consent at any time. Clearing this site's data in your browser resets your cookie choice immediately.

Data portability

You may receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format. Because the data is short plain text you sent us, we will simply send it back to you.

Lodge a complaint

You may complain to your local data protection authority. We would rather you told us first, so we can fix it, but that is your choice and not a precondition.

We do not carry out automated decision-making or profiling that produces legal or similarly significant effects. The advertising partners described in section 5 may do their own profiling; that is outside our control and is governed by their policies and, where it applies, the consent you gave.

12. Your rights under the CCPA and similar laws

If you are a California resident, or a resident of another state with similar legislation, you have the right to know what personal information is collected, to request deletion, to correct inaccurate information, to opt out of sale or sharing for cross-context behavioural advertising, and to limit use of sensitive personal information. We do not sell personal information and we do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising. You may exercise these rights without being discriminated against.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 16. Because we operate no accounts and ask for no identifiers, we have no practical way of linking any piece of information to a named individual for anyone who has not voluntarily sent us a bug report or message.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us as described in section 17. We will respond within the period the applicable law requires, and normally well before it.

13. Children

This service is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. A parent or guardian who believes a child has sent us information may contact us and ask for it to be deleted; we will do so.

14. Copyright and DMCA

SaveTube provides a tool. It hosts no video, holds no library, and has no catalogue. Media is resolved from the source platform and streamed on demand.

You are responsible for holding the right to download whatever you download. Downloading material you do not own or have permission to use may be unlawful in your jurisdiction, and it is always unlawful to re-upload someone else's work as your own.

Rights holders who believe material is being distributed through this service should note that we do not host it. Our full position and the procedure for reporting material is set out on our Copyright Policy.

15. Accuracy of this policy

This policy is written to match what the software actually does. Where it says the site stores no cookies of its own, that reflects the current implementation, not an intention. If the behaviour of the site changes, this page is updated in step with the code rather than afterwards.

16. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the service changes. The date at the top always reflects the current version. Material changes - a new category of data, a new advertising partner, a new sharing arrangement - will be called out rather than changed silently.

17. Contact and complaints
