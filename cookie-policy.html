Cookie Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This Cookie Policy explains what cookies are, how SaveTube uses them, and how you can control them. It applies to anyone visiting the website.

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device by your web browser when you visit a website. They allow the site to remember information between pages and between visits, such as your preferences. Similar technologies include local storage, web beacons, and pixel tags.

2. Cookies and storage we use

SaveTube uses a small number of cookies and browser storage features:

  • Consent preference (local storage): remembers whether you accepted or declined the cookie banner so we do not ask you again on every visit.
  • Unlock state (session storage): remembers, during your current visit, that you completed the unlock step so you do not see the gate again for the same session.
  • Analytics cookies: may be used to count visits and understand which pages are popular. This helps us keep the site simple and fast.
  • Advertising cookies: may be placed by our advertising partners to show ads and measure how they perform.

3. Advertising cookies from third parties

We may work with advertising networks such as Adsterra and PropellerAds. When you see an ad on this site, the ad network may set its own cookies. These cookies can be used to recognize your device, limit how often you see an ad, and build a profile of your interests across websites in order to show ads that are more relevant to you. These cookies are controlled by the ad network, not by us.

4. How to control cookies

You have several options:

  • When the cookie consent banner appears on your first visit, you can choose Accept or Decline.
  • You can change your browser settings to block or delete cookies. Instructions are available in the help section of every major browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge, and others).
  • You can use the opt-out tools provided by advertising networks, such as the ones from Adsterra and PropellerAds, to stop personalized advertising.
  • You can clear all site data from your browser, which removes the consent and unlock state.

If you block cookies, the site will still work, but the consent banner may appear again and some personalization may be reduced.

5. Changes to this policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page shows the latest version. Please check this page occasionally to stay informed.

6. Contact

If you have any questions about cookies or this policy, please contact us through the details shown on the site.
