Copyright Policy

What SaveTube is, what it does not host, and how rights holders can raise a concern.

1. What SaveTube is

SaveTube is a technical tool, not a media library. It does not host, upload, index, or store any videos or audio files. It does not keep a catalogue of other people's content. Every file is prepared for the requesting user on demand and the temporary copy is deleted after it is delivered.

2. No hosting, no library

Because SaveTube stores no media, there is no content library to search and nothing that stays on the site after a request is complete. All video and audio content remains on the original platform.

3. User responsibility

Users are responsible for what they download and how they use it. By using the site you agree that you will only save material you are entitled to save — such as content you own, content published under a licence that permits copying, content in the public domain, or content you have explicit permission to copy. SaveTube does not verify ownership and does not grant any rights.

4. Rights holders

If you are a copyright owner or an authorised agent and you believe the site is being used in a way that infringes your rights, you may send a notice to business.support.website@proton.me. To help us act quickly, include:

  • Your name, address, telephone number, and email address.
  • A description of the copyrighted work you believe is affected.
  • The exact URL on this site and any details that let us identify the request.
  • A statement that you have a good-faith belief the use is not authorised.
  • A statement, under penalty of perjury, that the information is accurate and that you are the owner or authorised to act for the owner.
  • Your physical or electronic signature.

5. What we do with a valid notice

Because no content is hosted, there is usually nothing to remove. Where a concern relates to how the service works, we will review it, investigate promptly, and take reasonable action, which may include changing or limiting functionality. We will reply to the sender with the outcome.

6. Repeat concerns

We take repeated or serious complaints seriously and may restrict use of the site where there is a pattern of abuse.

7. Counter-notice

If you believe a request made about you was mistaken, you may reply to the same address with an explanation. We will review it in good faith.

8. No affiliation

SaveTube is independent and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to YouTube, Google, or any other platform whose links it can process. All trademarks belong to their respective owners.

9. Contact

Copyright questions and notices: business.support.website@proton.me. General questions can go through the Contact page.
